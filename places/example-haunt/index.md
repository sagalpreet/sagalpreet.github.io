**This is an example, not a real place.** Delete the `places/example-haunt/`
folder once you have written a real one, then re-run
`python3 tools/build_travel.py`.

A **haunt** is somewhere you go back to often enough that no single visit stands
out — a home town, a relative's house, the same hill station every summer. It
has no date and no itinerary, just whatever you want to say about the place in
general.

Note that this one sits at the same coordinates as a stop on the example trip.
The builder merges them into one pin, and clicking it offers a chooser rather
than hiding one behind the other.
