Bio

I am a Pre-Doctoral Researcher at Google DeepMind in the Autonomous Agents team, advised by Dr. Rishi Saket and Dr. Aravindan Raghuveer. Previously I was a Member of Technical Staff at Oracle AI, working on speech generation. Before that I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Ropar (2019–2023) with a concentration in Artificial Intelligence, advised by Prof. Shweta Jain, Prof. Shashi Shekhar Jha and Prof. Abhinav Dhall.

My research interests lie in developing principled algorithms that let agents generalize and adapt continually. I am interested in systems that don't just memorize trajectories but achieve robust generalization to unseen physics or scenarios. My work anchors in Reinforcement Learning while drawing on optimization and learning theory to get past methodological bottlenecks in dynamic, data-scarce environments.

For more background, see my Resume or CV. I am always happy to discuss research, potential collaborations, or just chat about ML — reach out by email or on LinkedIn.
